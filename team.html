Meet our Calm Waters Team!

The Calm Waters Team

Allie Paschen

Founder and Licensed Massage Therapist #LMT22007175

"I have had a love for water my whole life. In my past I've spent a few summers working on scuba boats and finding my calm in the beautiful ocean waters. When I was introduced to float therapy in 2020 I was able to connect with myself again in a new and wonderful way. I hope that Lafayette loves float therapy and finds as much peace floating as I have.

When not at the float center, I might be enjoying time with family, enjoying good cuisine, writing and reading, or saving the world in video games."

Allie Is also a Licensed massage therapist and has trained at Ho'omana Spa in Maui, Hawaii under Kumu Jeana Iwalani Naluai in August of 2022.  Allie's massage focus is in Lomi Lomi , the traditional Hawaiian massage. Book a session with her on Tuesdays to experience massage techniques that have been passed down for generations.

Devin Paschen

Founder

"I am new to floating but with as busy as I am I look forward to unwinding with a sauna/ float combo! Like my wife Allie I've always loved water. Last year I even got my open water scuba to start exploring more of what we can't see from the surface.

I've been working at Anderson Heating & Cooling for the past 8 years and now work for Schuerich Plumbing Heating and Co , so with my background in the trades that makes me the designated maintenance and construction guy for our

small business.

In the evenings I also am the Twin Lakes High School Percussion Director. So when I'm not doing heating & cooling, fixing equipment and helping at Calm Waters, I'm sharing my love for music and percussion with my students!"
