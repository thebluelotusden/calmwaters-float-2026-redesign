Calm Waters Float Tanks

Our Royal Spa float pods offer a relaxing experience to step away from the noise, lights, and distractions from every day life.

Each float tank is heated to a cozy 94 degrees Fahrenheit to match your skin temperature so relaxing will be easy.  Each tank has around 1000 lbs of high-quality, medical grade, magnesium sulfate-also known as epsom salt. Epsom salt is a well known product that has been used for years to relieve pain, muscle aches, and soreness. With the high saturation of the epsom salt, floating is easy to achieve. Allow yourself a chance to experience weightlessness in our pods.

Our tanks have 3 different means of sanitization to ensure a clean and clear floating solution.

Our float tanks have an internal pod light you can turn on or off at anytime, and you can open or close the pod door ant anytime, meaning you are in total control of your session!

Enjoy a completely private experience as all of our float rooms have a private shower and materials provided for your float and shower.

INFARED SAUNA

When we think of sauna we traditionally think of the Finnish style sauna (lots of steam and high humidity). Infrared saunas vary from the Finnish style sauna mainly in design but also in how you experience the heat provided.  Instead of using steam, infrared saunas infrared heaters to produce radiant heat, which is then absorbed through the surface of the skin. So instead of high steamy temperatures infrared sauna uses a dry heat sitting at about 120-140 ' F.

Infrared Sauna is beneficial for aches and pains, menstrual cramps, boosting immune system, increasing circulation, reducing recovery time and so much more!

Our Infrared sauna fits up to two people, so you can book it for yourself, or bring a friend. We have a shower in your private sauna room so you can freshen up after your session!

Turn up the heat in our infrared sauna. Enjoy our sauna by yourself or with a friend.

Halo Therapy in our Halo Booth

 Welcome to our salt cave in a box!

Halo therapy (also know as salt cave therapy or dry-salt therapy) is an all natural breathing treatment.  The benefits of Halo Therapy are astounding. Halo therapy comes on the Greek word Halos for salt!

Halo therapy involves breathing in air infused with tiny, pharmaceutical-grade salt particles and is considered and a natural treatment for respiratory and skin conditions. Our Halo generator (the magic behind our salt booth and most salt caves) crushes the salt into tiny breathable particles and blows it into the air.

Halo therapy originated in the salt mines of Poland after mine workers spent hours breathing in the salty air and always felt better after working.

Our Halo Therapy booth has a full body medical grade red light therapy tower you can add as an upgrade, an Infrared Sauna component, and is also large enough to accommodate a rolled out yoga mat (anyone want a private halo hot yoga session?)

This service is perfect for families and safe for all ages. If you need relief from allergies, asthma, sinus issues, eczema, psoriasis, cough cold and congestion, then halo therapy is perfect for you!

It’s also great for helping clear up respiratory ailments, and is perfect  for cold and flu season, and allergy season! Perfect for when you or your littles ones need help kicking a sickness.
