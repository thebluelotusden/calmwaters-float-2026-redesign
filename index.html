Our Services

Click on any service below to explore its details and benefits.

Floating

Remove gravity and let your joints and bones truly rest. Step away from the noise and experience weightlessness.

  • Remove gravity and let your joints and bones truly rest
  • Release muscle tension
  • Reduce blood pressure
  • Reduce Heart Rate
  • Decrease the production of Cortisol (the stress hormone)
  • Brain can enter the theta-wave state, the state that our body goes into repair and rejuvenate mode

Infrared Sauna

Experience dry, radiant infrared heat that penetrates deep into the cells to ease tired muscles and detoxify.

  • Reduce blood pressure and increase heart health
  • Can help improve immune system
  • Better sleep cycles
  • Improve blood circulation and ease tired muscles

Massage Therapy

Let our licensed massage therapists help you melt physical tension and calm your nervous system.

  • Reduce pain and muscle soreness
  • Lower heart rate and blood pressure
  • Experience safe touch
  • Reduce stress and increase relaxation

Halo Dry Salt Therapy

Breathe easier in a dry-salt booth designed to enhance respiratory wellness and boost skin health.

  • Enhance respiratory health and boosts immune resilience
  • Facilitates detoxification, aids skin conditions (psoriasis, eczema, etc) and alleviates chronic pain
  • Ease muscle tension and stress, accelerate recovery and improve sport performance
  • Relieve and prevent colds and allergies
  • Safe and effective for all ages

Scalp Facials

Treat your scalp to a customized, pampering facial ritual that cleanses, hydrates, and melts tension.

  • Analyze the scalp and customize the session for any scalp concerns
  • Melt tension and relax in an ultimate pampering experience
  • Find new products for your hair and skin routines that are made of cleaner ingredients. Leave harsh chemicals from traditional hair and skin care behind!
All services are by appointment only!
